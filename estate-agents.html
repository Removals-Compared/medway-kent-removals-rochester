The removal firm your completions deserve

Every negotiator knows the feeling: the sale completes, and then the phone rings because the removal van is late, the price has grown on the doorstep, or the crew gave up waiting for keys. The remover is the last trade your client deals with, and their experience colours how they remember the whole move, including you.

We are the answer to that phone call. Medway and Kent Removals is a Rochester based, fully insured removal company working across the whole of Kent, seven days a week, with 5 stars from more than fifty Google reviews. Our prices are fixed and itemised before moving day, our crews are directly employed rather than subcontracted, and when completions run late our vehicle waits with the client’s belongings locked and insured, at no extra charge.

What your clients get

  • A quote within 60 minutes during business hours, itemised and fixed
  • Priority on confirmed completion dates, including the month-end crush
  • Patience with late keys: no waiting-time meter running against your client
  • A 10% deposit secures the date, the balance is payable on the move date
  • Full insurance: goods in transit and public liability on every job, proof on request
