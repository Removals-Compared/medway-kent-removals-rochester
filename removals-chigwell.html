Moving to or from Chigwell: routes, parking and the ULEZ question

Chigwell is one of the best connected villages in Essex, which is exactly why it moves so often. The Central line's Hainault loop serves Chigwell and Grange Hill stations for London commutes, the A113 runs the length of the district towards Abridge in one direction and the North Circular at Woodford in the other, and the M11 at Loughton links the whole area to the M25. For our crews coming up from Rochester, the Dartford Crossing makes IG7 a straightforward morning start, and those crossing charges are ours to absorb inside your quote, not yours to discover later.

Two local details catch people out. First, parking: Chigwell's housing extremes mean either a private driveway that fits a Luton with room to spare, or a station-side street where Epping Forest District Council permit rules apply and a suspension or considerate planning is needed. We confirm which situation applies at both ends of your move before quoting, and advise on council timelines where a suspension is worth arranging. Second, the ULEZ boundary: Chigwell itself is outside the Ultra Low Emission Zone, but the moment a move crosses into the London Borough of Redbridge at Hainault or Woodford, the zone applies. Our vehicles are compliant, so the answer for your wallet is simple: it is handled, and it is included.

Beyond local moves, the routes we run most from IG7 are into the Essex commuter towns, out to Kent, and the long-distance legs to the Midlands, the North and the South West. Moves between Chigwell and London boroughs happen weekly in both directions. Wherever the destination, it is one fixed quote, one crew, door to door.
