# Pricing — Medway & Kent Removals

Fixed price removal quotes across Medway and Kent. Every quote is itemised, all inclusive and held provided the property contents and access are as described. No hidden charges, no moving day surprises, no extra fees for stairs, parking waits or heavy items beyond what is stated in the quote.

Last reviewed: 15 September 2026

## House removals, local (within Medway: Rochester, Chatham, Gillingham, Strood, Rainham)

- 2 bedroom move: from £350 to £550
- 3 bedroom move: from £550 to £850
- 4 to 5 bedroom move: from £900 upwards

## House removals, East Kent (Thanet: Margate, Ramsgate, Broadstairs and nearby)

- 2 bedroom move within Thanet or to neighbouring East Kent towns: from £500 to £750
- 3 bedroom move: from £750 to £1,150
- Larger period family homes and moves with packing services will be higher

## Long distance removals

- Kent to anywhere in the UK, priced individually based on destination, volume and access
- Request a fixed quote by phone or the online form

## What every removal price includes

- Maxi loader Luton van and professional movers (crew sized to the job)
- Loading, transport and unloading
- Furniture blankets and protection
- Dismantling and reassembly of standard beds and flat pack furniture
- Goods in transit insurance (full replacement value)
- Public liability insurance
- Placement of items in the correct rooms

## Booking and payment terms

- Deposit: 10% of the quote value, payable on booking to secure the date
- Balance: remaining 90% payable on the move date
- Quotes are valid for 14 days from the date issued and subject to availability
- VAT: not applicable, the price quoted is the price paid

## Other services

- Packing service: full or partial packing with materials, priced per job
- Man and van: smaller moves, single item pickups and deliveries, priced per job
- Storage: secure short and long term storage, priced per week by volume
