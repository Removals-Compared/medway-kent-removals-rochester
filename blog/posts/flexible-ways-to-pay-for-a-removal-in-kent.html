Flexible Ways to Pay for a Removal in Kent, Including Klarna

By Amos Osho, Founder of Medway and Kent Removals | November 2026

Moving house rarely comes at a quiet moment. Solicitors, searches, deposits, new furniture and a dozen small bills all land at once, and the removal is often the one cost you can plan around. That is why we have built flexibility into how you pay. When you book online with Medway and Kent Removals you can pay a small deposit, pay the whole price by card, or pay with Klarna, which lets you split the cost into instalments.

This guide explains each option plainly, who it suits, and what to check before you choose. Flexible payment should make a move easier to afford, not harder to understand.

Three ways to pay when you book online

  • A 10% deposit: pay a tenth of your fixed price to secure the date, and the remaining 90% on moving day.
  • Pay in full by card: pay everything when you book, with nothing to pay on the day.
  • Pay with Klarna: pay for your move through Klarna and spread the cost over instalments, for smaller moves within the limit Klarna sets.

All three start from the same fixed price. There is no VAT added, the service is identical, and cancellation is free with everything you have paid refunded to the way you paid. You can see which options apply to your move on our instant quote and pay page, which only shows Klarna when it is available for your price.

Why flexible payment matters when you move

A removal is a large single bill at a stressful time. Even people with good savings can find that the weeks around completion are tight, because money is committed before it is released. Sale proceeds arrive on completion day, but the removal firm needs a date secured in advance. Flexible payment closes that gap.

It also helps people who are not buying or selling at all. Renters moving between tenancies face a new deposit and first month's rent just as the removal falls due. Students, families relocating for work and people moving a parent into care all have their own pressure points. Having more than one way to pay means you can pick the one that fits your month.

How paying with Klarna works

Klarna is a payment provider that lets you pay for a purchase in instalments instead of all at once. When you reach checkout on our site you choose Klarna as your payment method, and Klarna shows you the instalment options available for your order and what each payment would be. You choose, confirm, and your move is booked.

A few points are worth knowing before you choose it.

  • It is credit provided by Klarna. Klarna, not Medway and Kent Removals, provides and manages it, and you agree to Klarna's terms when you use it.
  • You must be 18 or over, and use is subject to Klarna's approval. Not everyone will be approved, and that decision is Klarna's.
  • The options shown at checkout are the ones that apply to you. The number of instalments and their timing come from Klarna, so always read what is on screen before you confirm.
  • Missing a payment has consequences. Klarna's terms explain what happens if an instalment is late, so make sure you are comfortable with the repayments first.

Who Klarna suits

Klarna tends to suit people who want the removal booked and paid for now, but would rather not take the whole amount from their account in one go. It can work well if you have regular income, expect to be a little stretched around completion, and like knowing the schedule in advance.

It is less suitable if you are already managing several credit commitments, or if you would find a missed payment difficult. In that case a 10% deposit with the balance on the day, or paying in full from savings, may be kinder to your budget. Only take on instalments you are confident you can meet.

Who the deposit suits

The 10% deposit is the simplest flexible option, because it involves no credit at all. You pay a tenth to secure your date and settle the other 90% on moving day by card, bank transfer or cash. It suits people whose money arrives on completion day, and people who prefer to see the job done before paying most of it. Our guide on choosing between a deposit and paying in full goes through this in detail.

Who should pay in full by card

Paying in full by card suits people who want a single payment, a single receipt and nothing to think about on moving day. It also suits anyone moving on behalf of someone else, or claiming the cost back from an employer. A card payment can also give you extra routes to resolve a problem through your card provider, should one ever arise.

What stays the same whichever you choose

Choosing a flexible payment option does not change the move. The price is fixed. The same crew and vehicles arrive. Your goods are covered by goods in transit insurance, and public liability insurance applies. Nothing about the service is better or worse depending on how you pay.

The same free cancellation applies too. If your plans change, everything you have paid is refunded to the original payment method. With Klarna, the refund goes back through Klarna and your unpaid instalments are cancelled. It can take Klarna a few days to show that in your account.

A simple way to choose

  1. Look at the weeks around your move. When is money going out, and when does it arrive?
  2. Decide how you feel about credit. If you prefer to avoid it, choose the deposit or pay in full.
  3. Check the offer on screen. If you choose Klarna, read the instalment plan carefully before you confirm.
  4. Put the dates in your diary. Whatever you choose, note when each payment is due.

Questions to ask about any payment plan

These questions apply whether you are using Klarna, another finance provider or a removal company's own arrangement.

  • Who is providing the credit, and whose terms apply?
  • When is each payment taken, and from where?
  • What happens if a payment is missed?
  • What happens to the plan if I cancel or change my date?
  • Is the removal price the same whichever way I pay?

A trustworthy company will answer all of these clearly. If you cannot get a straight answer, treat that as a warning.

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay for my removal with Klarna?

Yes, for smaller moves within the limit Klarna sets. Klarna is shown at checkout when it is available for your price. It is subject to Klarna's approval and terms, and you must be 18 or over.

Does the price change if I use Klarna?

No. The price is the same whichever way you pay, and we do not add a charge for choosing Klarna.

Can I pay a deposit instead?

Yes. A 10% deposit secures your date and the remaining 90% is paid to the crew on moving day.

What if I cancel after paying with Klarna?

Cancellation is free. The refund goes back through Klarna and any unpaid instalments are cancelled, though it can take Klarna a few days to show.

Is there VAT on top?

No. VAT is not applicable, so the price quoted is the price you pay.
