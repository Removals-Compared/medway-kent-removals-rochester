Deposit or Pay in Full: Which Suits Your Move?

By Amos Osho, Founder of Medway and Kent Removals | October 2026

When you book a removal online with Medway and Kent Removals you can pay in two main ways. You can put down a 10% deposit and pay the rest on moving day, or you can pay the whole price when you book. Neither is better for everyone. This guide helps you decide which suits your move, your budget and the way you like to organise things.

The two options side by side

Both options start the same way: you get a fixed price and choose your date. After that, here is how they differ.

  • Deposit: you pay 10% of the price now. The other 90% goes to the crew on moving day, by card, bank transfer or cash.
  • Pay in full: you pay the whole price now by card. On moving day there is nothing left to pay.

In both cases the price is fixed, there is no VAT added, and cancellation is free with everything paid refunded to the original payment method. That last point is worth repeating, because it means that paying in full does not lock you in. The money is yours to get back if your plans change.

Why people choose the deposit

The deposit is the most popular choice, and for good reasons.

It keeps cash free for everything else. A house move is expensive in ways that have nothing to do with the van. Solicitors, searches, stamp duty, deposits on a new tenancy, new curtains, a broken boiler on the first night. Spreading the removal cost so that only a tenth leaves your account now can make the weeks before completion much easier.

It lets you see the job done before you pay most of it. Some people simply feel better handing over the balance once the crew has turned up and the first boxes are on the van. That is a perfectly reasonable instinct.

It suits moves where money arrives late. If you are selling and buying, the proceeds of your sale may not reach you until completion day itself. Paying a small deposit now and the rest from those proceeds can fit the timing neatly.

Why people choose to pay in full

Paying in full has its own advantages, and they are mostly about peace of mind.

One less thing to think about on the day. Moving day is busy. There are keys to collect, utilities to read, children and pets to keep out of the way. Having nothing left to pay removes a task from the list and means you do not need to have cash or your card to hand when the crew finish.

No chance of a missed balance. If you are away from the property, being managed by an agent, or moving a relative who cannot easily handle payments, paying in advance avoids confusion on the day.

It suits company and landlord moves. If someone else is covering the cost, a single payment and a single confirmation makes it easier to claim the expense or pass the receipt on.

You may like the tidy paper trail. One payment and one confirmation email can be easier to file than a deposit and a separate balance.

Where Klarna fits in

There is a third way to pay for smaller moves. When you book online you can pay the whole price with Klarna instead of paying by card, as long as the total is within the limit Klarna sets for the service. Klarna splits the payment into instalments, and it is subject to Klarna's approval and terms, with an 18+ age limit. It is a form of credit provided by Klarna, so read their terms before choosing it, and only use it if you are comfortable with the repayments.

A simple way to decide

If you are still unsure, run through these questions.

  1. Is your date firm? If it is not, a deposit keeps less money in play. Cancellation is free either way, but a smaller payment is less to wait for if it needs refunding.
  2. Is cash tight before completion? Pick the deposit and pay the balance from your sale proceeds or your next paycheque.
  3. Do you hate loose ends on the day? Pay in full and enjoy a calmer morning.
  4. Is someone else paying? Pay in full so that they get one clear receipt.
  5. Do you want to keep the option of paying cash or by bank transfer for the balance? Choose the deposit, since the balance can be settled in any of those ways.

What does not change

Whichever you choose, the following stays the same, and these are the things that matter most.

  • The price is fixed. It will not go up on the day for the work you have described.
  • The same crew, the same vans and the same service apply, whether you paid 10% or 100%.
  • You receive a confirmation email showing what you have paid and what, if anything, is left.
  • Cancellation is free, and your money comes back the way it went in.

Choosing to pay in full does not buy a better service, and choosing the deposit does not mean a lesser one. It is purely a question of what is convenient for you.

Paying the balance if you chose the deposit

If you pay a deposit, the remaining 90% is due on the move date, paid to the crew. You can use a card, a bank transfer or cash. It is a good idea to decide which one you will use before the day, so that you are not searching for a card reader or a cash machine while the van is being loaded. Our guide to paying the balance on moving day walks through how that works.

Tips whichever you pick

  • Check the confirmation email as soon as it arrives. Make sure the date, the postcodes and the property size are right.
  • Keep the email until your belongings are in your new home and everything is unpacked and checked.
  • If anything on the booking is wrong, tell us straight away so that it can be corrected before the day.
  • If you need extras, such as packing, ask early. Packing is a separate service, booked after a survey.

A worked example in plain terms

Imagine you have a fixed price for a two bedroom move. If you choose the deposit, 10% is taken when you book, and the other 90% is due on moving day. If you choose to pay in full, the whole price is taken when you book, and the crew simply arrive and get on with the job. At the end, the total you have paid is identical. The only thing that changes is the timing, and the effort of handling a payment on the day.

That is why we suggest thinking about your own circumstances rather than the options themselves. Ask what your next few weeks look like, what else you must pay for, and how you feel about handling money on a busy day. The right choice is nearly always obvious once you put it that way.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change from a deposit to paying in full later?

Yes, speak to us. If you would like to settle the balance before moving day, we can arrange it by phone.

Is there a discount for paying in full?

No. The price is the same whichever way you pay, which keeps things simple and fair.

Is paying in full safe?

Payments are taken by Stripe, a major payment provider, and refunded to the original method if you cancel. As with any company, check their details first, and keep your confirmation.

What if my move costs more than expected?

The online price is fixed for the job you describe. For journeys of five hours or more, we confirm a fixed price with you directly. If the job changes materially, we talk to you before anything is added.
