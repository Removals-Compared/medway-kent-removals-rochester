Changing Your Moving Date: What Happens to Your Payment?

By Amos Osho, Founder of Medway and Kent Removals | October 2026

Moving dates are not always as fixed as they look. A buyer drops out, a mortgage offer is delayed, a surveyor finds a problem, or a chain simply takes longer than anyone hoped. If you have already booked and paid for your removal, the natural worry is what happens to the money. This guide explains what to do, how to protect yourself, and how payments are handled when you book with Medway and Kent Removals.

Do not wait to tell your removal company

The single most useful thing you can do is let your removal company know as soon as you think your date might move. You do not need to have a new date. Even a simple message saying that completion may be delayed gives the company time to plan, and it often opens up more options than you would have if you waited until the last minute.

The reason is practical. A removal day is a slot in a diary. The earlier a slot is released, the more likely it can be offered to someone else, and the more likely we can find you a good alternative. If you tell us the day before, there is far less we can do.

Two different situations

There are two quite different cases, and the money works differently in each.

1. You want to move to a new date

If your move is still going ahead and only the day is changing, get in touch and we will look at the availability for your new date. Any new date is subject to availability, which is why speaking to us early helps. Your booking, your fixed price and what you have already paid can usually be carried across to the new date, rather than you paying twice.

2. You need to cancel the move

If the move is off altogether, cancellation is free. Everything you have paid is refunded in full to the payment method you used. This is true whether you paid the 10% deposit or paid in full.

How the refund reaches you

A refund is returned the way it came in, so the route depends on how you paid.

  • Card: the refund goes back to the card you used. It normally takes a few working days to appear, depending on your bank.
  • Klarna: the refund goes back through Klarna, and any instalments you have not yet paid are cancelled. It can take Klarna a few days to show the change in your account.
  • Bank transfer or cash: if you paid a balance this way, we arrange the return with you directly and give you a written record.

If a refund is slow, check with your bank or with Klarna first, because the delay is often on the receiving side rather than ours. If you are still unsure, call us and we will confirm what has been sent.

Protecting yourself when your date is uncertain

Most of the stress comes from booking too firmly too early. Here are sensible ways to protect yourself.

  1. Wait for exchange if you can. Until contracts are exchanged, a completion date is a hope rather than a fact. If you can wait to book, do so.
  2. If you must book early, choose the smaller payment. A 10% deposit keeps less money tied up while your date is uncertain.
  3. Check the cancellation terms in writing. With any company, get the policy by email before paying.
  4. Say what is uncertain. Tell the company that your date depends on a chain. A good one will help you plan around it.
  5. Keep a nearby second date in mind. If you know a couple of dates that might work, mention them.

If completion is delayed on the day itself

Sometimes the problem appears on moving day, when the crew are already loading or about to leave. Completion may be late in the afternoon, the keys may be delayed, or the sale may not complete at all. Call us straight away. We can often wait, hold your belongings safely on the van, or arrange storage and redeliver the next morning. These are practical decisions, and the sooner we know, the more options there are. Our guide to what actually happens on completion day explains the timing in more detail.

What about extras you booked?

If you have also asked about packing, or other services booked alongside the move, tell us about those too. Packing is a separate service arranged after a survey, so it is handled separately from the move itself. Change one and let us know about the other, so that nothing is left booked for a day you no longer need.

Keeping your paperwork straight

When dates move, paperwork gets muddled. A few habits help.

  • Keep the original confirmation email, even after the date changes.
  • Ask for a short written confirmation of the new date, and check that it shows what you have paid.
  • If you are refunded, note the date and the amount, and check it against your bank or Klarna statement.
  • If you used a card, hold on to the receipt until the refund has landed.

What a fair policy looks like

If you are comparing companies, look for a policy that is easy to find, written in plain English, and clear about three things: what happens if you cancel, what happens if you move your date, and what happens if the company cannot make the day. Vague wording is a reason to ask more questions. Policies that quietly keep your deposit, or that change the price when the date changes, deserve extra care.

Why we keep it simple

We would rather you booked with confidence than with a worry in the back of your mind. Free cancellation, a fixed price and clear confirmations are how we try to take the risk out of booking early. If your date moves, your first step is simply to talk to us.

What to say when you get in touch

A short, clear message makes it much quicker to help you. When you contact us about a change, it helps to include the following.

  • Your name and the booking reference from your confirmation email.
  • The date you originally booked.
  • Whether you want to move to a new date or cancel altogether.
  • Any dates that might work, if you have a few in mind.
  • Whether the reason is a delay you expect to be short or one that could run for weeks.

With that in front of us, we can check the diary and come back to you with real options instead of a list of questions. If you are not sure of the new date, say so. A rough window, such as the last week of the month, is still useful.

Why early notice is fair to everyone

A removal slot cannot be recovered once it has passed. When a customer tells us early that a date is changing, another household can often take that slot, and the customer who moves it gets a better choice of alternatives. When notice comes late, the day is lost for everyone. We make cancellation free so that nobody feels they must hold on to a date they cannot keep, and we hope that makes early notice an easy habit.

Frequently asked questions

Will I lose my deposit if I change the date?

No. If your move is going ahead on another date, your booking can be carried across, subject to availability. If you cancel, everything you paid is refunded.

Can I rebook later without a new quote?

Quotes are valid for 14 days from the date they are issued. After that we may need to confirm the price again, so ask us when you are ready.

What if the sale falls through completely?

Cancel with us and the money you paid goes back to the way you paid. You can book again when you have a new date.

How do I change my date?
